Our Story

Where every meal
feels like home

Pisces by Senani is Raebareli's beloved dining destination — a place where vibrant flavours meet warm hospitality. Nestled on Manika Cinema Road, we bring you an all-you-can-eat experience that celebrates the richness of Indian cuisine alongside refreshing café delights.

Whether you're gathering with family around our fireplace on a cool evening or enjoying a leisurely afternoon on our outdoor terrace, every visit is crafted to be memorable. Come hungry, leave happy.
